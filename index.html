Privacy Policy for Jump on Color

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Aton Tech Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Jump on Color (the "Application"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Jump on Color does not require user registration, account creation, or login credentials. We do not directly collect or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).

Game progress, preferences, unlocked items, coins, and high scores are saved locally on your device using local storage.

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics, performance, and advertising purposes:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Used to display banner and rewarded video advertisements within the game. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs (AAID), IP addresses, and app interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Safety & Security

We value your trust. All network communications between the Application and third-party advertising SDKs are transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS connections. We do not sell or share personal data with unauthorized third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

5. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
